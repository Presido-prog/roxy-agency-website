First time only

You need a free GitHub account

The public site already works. Saving edits from your phone starts after GitHub (and a free host) are connected. Do not pay for anything. Pages CMS is free.

  1. Create a free GitHub account at github.com/signup with an email you can open on this phone.
  2. Ask your helper to upload this website folder to a new GitHub repository and turn on automatic publishing (GitHub Pages or Netlify). The steps are in the project README.
  3. Open app.pagescms.org, sign in with GitHub, and install the Pages CMS app on that repository only.
  4. On your phone, open the editor, tap the three-dot menu in the browser, and choose Add to Home screen so it feels like an app.

Every day

How to change the site

  1. Open Pages CMS from your home screen (or go to app.pagescms.org) and sign in with GitHub if asked.
  2. Open the Roxy repository.
  3. Pick a section: Homepage headline, Events, Salary table, Photo gallery, Videos, and so on.
  4. Change the text, or tap a photo/video field and choose a picture or clip from your phone. Keep videos under 8 MB.
  5. Tap Save (or the save/commit button). Wait one or two minutes, then refresh the public website.

What lives where

  • Site name & contact — WhatsApp, email, invite link, footer.
  • Homepage headline — the big welcome and the hero photos.
  • How to join and Academy cards — steps and training.
  • Salary table and Invite & earn — numbers and commission.
  • Events — add or hide a poster/event card.
  • Photo gallery — add pictures from your phone.
  • Videos — swap or add a short video.
  • Host stories — only real quotes, with permission. Leave empty to keep “Your story here.”
